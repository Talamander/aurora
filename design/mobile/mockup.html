Profiling Python with the new sampling profiler

Python 3.15 ships a sampling profiler in the standard library. I pointed it at a slow Datasette query and got a flame graph in about four seconds.

The interesting part is that it attaches to a running process. You do not restart anything, you do not add a decorator, you give it a PID and it starts sampling stacks at a thousand hertz.

Most of the time went to JSON encoding, which I would never have guessed.
